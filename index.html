About me

I am a career-changing developer with a background in healthcare. For years I worked as a Certified Nursing Assistant, supporting people in their homes and in care facilities. That work taught me patience, attention to detail, and how to stay calm when things get hard.

I am now focused on software. I started with HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, and I am deepening my JavaScript while I practice by shipping small sites. My goal is to grow into a software engineer who can build useful, reliable web applications.
